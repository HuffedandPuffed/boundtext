Finn and the Secret Path

Book one in the Finn Adventure series - ages 4 to 8

Finn loves his morning walks in the Queensland bush, until he finds a narrow sandy path that leads to the edge of a huge canyon full of giant yellow machines - the mine where his mum and dad work. Join Finn at Sunny Creek Mine to see the haul trucks, the dragline and the shining black coal, follow it all the way onto a train that stretches as far as the eye can see, and find out how the land is put back at the end. A gentle, illustrated introduction to mining for curious young readers who always ask "how does that work?"

Authors

Colin Bamforth

Publisher, Practical Nonfiction Author & Puzzle-Book Creator

Colin Bamforth is the visionary behind Bound Text Publishing. Operating at the intersection of structure and creativity, Colin is a dedicated practical nonfiction author and puzzle-book creator. Driven by a mission to champion human voices and amplify 'useful sparks,' he curates and designs books that engage the mind, challenge curious readers, and bring high-quality independent publishing to life.
